# Jenson Bikes SQL Business Analysis

## Project Overview

Analyzed Jenson Bikes' relational database using SQL to evaluate store performance, customer purchasing behavior, product sales, staff performance, and overall business growth.

## Objectives

- Analyze store-wise sales and performance
- Evaluate customer purchasing behavior
- Identify top-selling products and categories
- Analyze staff performance
- Identify operational and product-level gaps
- Generate business insights using SQL

## Tools & Skills

- SQL
- Relational Database Analysis
- JOINs
- GROUP BY
- HAVING
- Subqueries
- Window Functions
- EXISTS / NOT EXISTS
- Aggregate Functions
- Filtering & Sorting

## Key Analysis

- Analyzed store-wise product sales and overall store performance.
- Identified high-value and frequent customers based on purchasing behavior.
- Evaluated top-selling products and category-level sales performance.
- Analyzed staff performance based on orders handled and sales contribution.
- Identified staff members with no handled orders.
- Identified products that have never been ordered.
- Used ranking and aggregation techniques to compare stores, products, customers, and staff.
- Performed relational analysis across customers, orders, products, stores, and staff data.

## Business Insights

The analysis provided insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product demand, store efficiency, and staff productivity to support data-driven business decisions.
